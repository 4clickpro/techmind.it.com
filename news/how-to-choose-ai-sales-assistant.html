How to Choose an AI Sales Assistant Without Losing Control of Your Leads

A buyer's guide to AI sales assistants covering lead qualification, CRM integration, booking, oversight and ROI.

Define the sales job first

Decide whether you need faster first responses, lead qualification, appointment scheduling, CRM updates or follow-up drafting. Each job requires different integrations and oversight.

Questions to ask before buying

Can the system identify when it is uncertain? Does it hand off urgent or sensitive calls? Can staff edit scripts, review conversations and correct CRM records? Does it explain which data it stores and for how long?

Design the lead workflow

A practical flow is inquiry received, consent and contact details captured, qualification questions asked, lead routed to the right person, and follow-up drafted for approval. Avoid promising bookings until calendar integration has been tested.

Track outcomes

Measure inquiries answered, qualified leads, appointments booked, show rate and sales closed. Compare these against staffing time and software costs. Review errors weekly.
