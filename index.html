Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): how it works and what is included

AEO gets TechMind quoted and cited by AI answers — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot and Gemini — so buyers find you in the answer, not just in the search results.

  1. Step 1

    Send your URL

    That is all we need to start. No lengthy setup or questionnaire.

  2. Step 2

    Get your AI Visibility Audit

    Within 48 business hours: how AI engines describe and recommend you, who they recommend instead, verbatim answer evidence, prioritized fixes and a 60-day plan.

  3. Step 3

    Run the AEO Fix Sprint

    We do the fixes for you, then measure the agreed AI-visibility metrics over 60 days.

What is included

  • AI crawler access — your site opened to the crawlers that power AI answers (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and others), set in robots.txt
  • Sitemap and llms.txt, so AI engines can read what you offer
  • Structured data / schema on your key pages
  • Answer-first pages that state the answer plainly
  • Citation tracking and reporting — real citations, not promises

What AEO is not

  • No guaranteed citation, ranking or placement — the engines decide
  • No fake mentions or reviews
  • Not a one-time trick — it is built on real, helpful content

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Perplexity?

Start with an AI Visibility Audit. Send TechMind your website URL and we test how ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI answers describe and recommend your business, who they recommend instead, and what to fix first. The AEO Fix Sprint then does the fixes for you.

The first 10 businesses get it free, then it is $99. You get a snapshot of how AI engines describe and recommend your business, who they recommend instead, verbatim answer evidence, prioritized fixes and a 60-day plan, delivered within 48 business hours.

What is the AEO Fix Sprint guarantee?

The AEO Fix Sprint is $2,000 and comes with a 60-day results guarantee: if the agreed AI-visibility metrics do not improve measurably within 60 days, you get a full refund. It is a refund promise, not a guaranteed ranking or placement. The engines decide what they cite.

How many AI agents should I start with?

Start with one agent. The Focus package is one dedicated AI sales assistant. As your business needs grow, expand to Team (2 agents), Department (3), Operations (4–6), or an Enterprise AI Team (7–10).

What is AEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) helps AI engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot and Gemini — quote and cite your business in their answers. TechMind’s AEO package covers AI crawler access, sitemap, llms.txt, structured data, answer-first pages, and citation tracking. No citation, ranking or placement is guaranteed.
